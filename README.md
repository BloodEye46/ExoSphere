# ExoSphere &mdash; Infinite Frontiers

A stylized, offline-first mobile sci-fi colony, idle, and strategy game built with Three.js r128, Web Audio API, and high-performance HTML5 Canvas.

## Features

- **3D Procedural Worlds:** Low-poly procedural planets with dynamic biome generation (Temperate, Ocean, Desert, Ice, Volcanic, Toxic Jungle, Crystal, Barren), atmospheric scattering, cloud layers, and rings.
- **Capital City Diorama:** Seamless cinematic camera dive into the planet's surface revealing a 2.5D isometric hex grid. Build Habitats, Farms, Fusion Reactors, Labs, Recyclers, Clinics, Parks, Spaceports, and Defense Batteries with live adjacency energy streams and ambient cargo drones.
- **Life Support & Interplanetary Logistics:** Water and food management per colony. Water deficits trigger drought warnings, halting population growth. Spaceport supply freighter routes ship water and food from water worlds to arid or volcanic colonies.
- **The Tower &mdash; Infinite Skill Mandala:** Interactive pannable & zoomable radial skill tree with 5 radiant disciplines (*Industry, Society, Logistics, Military, Science*), 6 concentric technological tiers, live energy circuit pulses, and infinite outer singularities.
- **Spiral Galaxy Star Chart:** 420 star systems arranged along 4 galactic spiral arms with spectral glows, scanner radar radar domes radiating from player colonies, fog of war, and hyperspace warp transitions.
- **Tactical Sector Defense:** Endless wave defense battler featuring hero flagship thrusters, reactive energy shields, auto-firing dual pulse lasers, guided missile swarms, EMP shockwaves, and magnetizing Dark Matter pickups.
- **Procedural Alien Life & Xenodex:** Procedural alien creature generator with distinct anatomies (blobs, crawlers, mantas, colossi), holographic scanning, and permanent empire-wide buffs.
- **Procedural Web Audio Engine:** Zero external sound dependencies; 100% offline procedural audio synthesizer for clicks, chimes, laser blasts, explosions, and warp drives.

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
To package for mobile, wrap with **Capacitor** or an Android `WebView` shell.
